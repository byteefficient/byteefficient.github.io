## About Second Home

Second Home helps you track everything in your home — from pantry staples to cleaning supplies. Know what you have, what's running low, and what you need to buy.

- Track inventory across multiple homes
- Share homes with household members
- Scan barcodes for quick item entry
- Smart shopping list with one-tap restocking
- Works offline, syncs automatically
