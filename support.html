Common Questions

How do I share a home with someone?

Go to Settings > Manage Members, then tap Invite to generate a shareable code. The other person enters the code in Settings > Join a Home.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and all associated data from our servers immediately.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Second Home stores all data locally on your device. You can view and edit your inventory without an internet connection. Changes sync automatically when you're back online.

How do I scan a barcode?

Tap the + button on the home screen, then tap the barcode icon. Point your camera at a product barcode and the app will look up the product name automatically.

How do I export my data?

Go to Settings > Export as CSV to generate a CSV file of your current home's inventory. You can share it via email, Messages, or any other app.
